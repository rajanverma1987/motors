# Customer demo follow-ups

Notes from the customer demo. Each item is a requested capability or improvement.

## Dashboard

### Split by KPIs

Break the main dashboard into clear KPI sections so the shop can see performance at a glance (jobs, revenue, aging, inventory, and similar metrics), not one mixed overview.

## Purchasing and inventory

### Shop PO items to inventory

When a shop purchase order is received, line items should flow into inventory (receive against PO and increase stock), instead of staying only on the SHOP PO.

### Barcode / QR code scanning for inventory

Support scanning barcodes or QR codes to find, receive, issue, or count inventory items faster on the floor or tablet.

### Consumable inventory management (in / out)

Track consumables (tape, solder, packaging, shop supplies, and similar) with clear check-in and check-out (or issue) so usage and remaining stock stay visible.

### Purchase order due notification

Notify the right people when a purchase order is approaching or past its expected due / delivery date.

### Future inventory receiving follow-up

If inventory is expected to arrive later (for example about three months out), send a follow-up notification so the shop can check status with the vendor before it is overdue.

## Job work and reports



### Email AC / DC report to customer

Allow sending the AC / DC (test / inspection) report to the customer by email from the job or report flow.

### Add attached images in the printed report

Include photos or documents attached to the job in the printed report, so the customer PDF / printout shows the same images.

### Track employee hours on a job number

Record and report time each employee spends on a specific job number, for labor cost, payroll, and job profitability.

### Overdue status report

A report (or dashboard view) of overdue work: jobs, POs, or other items past due, with status so the shop can chase and prioritize.

### Connection drawing

Support connection drawings on the job (create, attach, or print), similar to how other job diagrams / drawings are used in the shop workflow.

## Access control



### Employee-level restriction of financials

Restrict financial information on screens and data (costs, margins, pricing, AP / AR, and similar) by employee role or permission, so only authorized staff see financial information.
